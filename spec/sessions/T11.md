## Approach
Ranks are computed by longest path (cycles broken by dropping DFS back edges; podtipovi of one spec share a rank; edges
oriented supertype -> S -> junction -> podtipovi). ELK gets the rank as `elk.partitioning.partition` (layers = ranks), and
supplies ordering and x. Both paths then share `finalize`: per-layer overlap resolution, junction centred over its
podtipovi, S centred over junction, y per layer (top aligned, so podtipovi share one y). ELK failure falls back to sync.
