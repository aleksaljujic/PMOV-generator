## Findings
- ELK 0.9.3 with `wrapping.strategy = MULTI_EDGE` ignores `layerChoiceConstraint` and may wrap a veza chain into side bands,
  so a veza is no longer between its participants in y. Hence the three-step fallback (wrapped ELK, unwrapped ELK, rank layout).
- The star fixture has 10 plain objekat neighbours (no vezas); one rank of 10 cards is wider than 3:1 in ELK, so it uses the rank layout.
